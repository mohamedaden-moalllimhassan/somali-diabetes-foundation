Research & Data · SDF

Evidence for better diabetes care.

SDF supports research and data collection to improve understanding of diabetes, inform health interventions and contribute to stronger diabetes policies and services in Somalia.

Our Research Commitment

Turning knowledge into meaningful action.

Research is an important part of SDF's approach to understanding the diabetes burden, identifying healthcare challenges and generating evidence that can support effective programs and decision-making.

R

Research & Data Collection

SDF aims to support research that can contribute to better understanding of diabetes prevalence, risk factors, healthcare challenges and opportunities for improving diabetes care in Somalia.

Research Priorities

Areas where evidence can strengthen the diabetes response.

SDF's research and data agenda is aligned with its organizational goals and the need for better information to guide diabetes prevention, care and health-system development.

01

Diabetes Epidemiology

Improving understanding of diabetes within Somali communities and identifying important patterns and risk factors.

  • Diabetes prevalence research.
  • Assessment of diabetes risk factors.
  • Community and population-level research.
02

Diabetes Risk & Prevention

Generating evidence that can support effective prevention strategies and healthier communities.

  • Identification of modifiable risk factors.
  • Research supporting prevention programs.
  • Evidence for community health education.
03

Healthcare Challenges

Understanding challenges affecting access to diabetes prevention, diagnosis, treatment and ongoing care.

  • Healthcare access and service delivery.
  • Diabetes treatment challenges.
  • Health-system research.
04

Diabetes Data Systems

Supporting improved collection and use of diabetes information for planning, monitoring and decision-making.

  • Strengthening diabetes data collection.
  • Exploring a national diabetes registry.
  • Supporting evidence-based planning.
