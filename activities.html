SCREENING & PREVENTION

Banadir Diabetes Awareness and Screening Initiative

The Banadir Diabetes Awareness and Screening Initiative (BDASI) focused on diabetes awareness, screening, assessment, community outreach and patient-focused activities. The activity period shown in SDF materials was 1 January–30 June 2025.

BDASI

Diabetes Awareness and Screening in the Community

BDASI brought together awareness, screening, diabetes assessment, community outreach and health-team activities as part of SDF's work to strengthen diabetes prevention and early detection.

The initiative was funded by CAA and implemented by the Somali Diabetes Foundation.
