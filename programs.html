What We Do · SDF

Our Programs

SDF brings together awareness, prevention, screening, education, advocacy, research, patient support and resource mobilization to strengthen the diabetes response in Somalia.

Our Program Areas

A comprehensive approach to diabetes.

The Somali Diabetes Foundation's programs are designed to address diabetes from prevention and early detection through education, healthcare capacity, policy, research and support for people living with diabetes and their families.

01
A

Public Awareness & Education

Increasing community knowledge about diabetes, prevention, healthy lifestyles and diabetes management.

  • Awareness campaigns through radio, television and social media.
  • Community outreach on healthy lifestyles and diabetes care.
  • Educational materials in local languages.
02
✓

Diabetes Screening & Prevention

Promoting risk assessment, early detection and prevention of diabetes and its complications.

  • Community-based diabetes screening programs.
  • Early detection and timely intervention.
  • Promotion of healthy eating and physical activity.
03
+

Healthcare Professional Training

Strengthening the knowledge and skills of healthcare professionals involved in diabetes prevention and care.

  • Training sessions and workshops for doctors, nurses and community health workers.
  • Continuing medical education opportunities.
  • Development of clinical guidelines and best practices.
04
⚖

Policy Advocacy & Health-System Strengthening

Working with stakeholders to strengthen diabetes policies, services and access to care.

  • Engagement with government health agencies.
  • Advocacy for affordable and accessible diabetes medications and treatment.
  • Supporting integration of diabetes care into primary healthcare services.
05
R

Research & Data Collection

Generating and using evidence to better understand diabetes and inform health policies and interventions.

  • Epidemiological studies on diabetes prevalence and risk factors.
  • Development of a national diabetes registry.
  • Publication of research findings and recommendations.
06
♥

Patient Support & Community Engagement

Supporting people living with diabetes and their families through education, guidance and community engagement.

  • Diabetes support groups for patients and families.
  • Peer mentoring for newly diagnosed patients.
  • Counselling and nutritional guidance.
07
$

Fundraising & Resource Mobilization

Mobilizing resources and partnerships to support sustainable diabetes programs and initiatives.

  • Securing funding through grants, donations and partnerships with international health organizations.
  • Organizing fundraising activities to support diabetes programs and initiatives.
  • Developing mission-aligned income-generating activities to strengthen financial sustainability.
Our Approach

Collaboration is at the heart of our work.

Communities

We engage communities directly through awareness, screening, education and support, with particular attention to people who face barriers to healthcare.

Healthcare Professionals

We support healthcare professionals through training, education and collaboration to strengthen diabetes care.

Institutions & Partners

We collaborate with government agencies, healthcare institutions, organizations and other stakeholders to strengthen the diabetes response.
