Our Work

Programs for a Healthier Somalia

The Somali Diabetes Foundation works across communities, healthcare settings, and institutions to strengthen diabetes prevention, awareness, screening, care, research, education, advocacy, and support.

What We Do

Turning awareness into action

Diabetes affects individuals, families, communities, and health systems. SDF develops and supports practical programs that respond to the needs of people at risk of diabetes and those already living with the condition.

Our work brings together prevention, early detection, patient support, healthcare professional development, research, advocacy, and community engagement.

Our program approach

We aim to connect communities, healthcare professionals, people living with diabetes, researchers, institutions, partners, and policymakers around a shared goal: improving diabetes prevention and care in Somalia.

Our Core Programs

Six areas of action

Our programs are designed to address diabetes across the prevention, detection, care, education, evidence, and health-system continuum.

01
A

Public Awareness & Education

We promote accurate, accessible information about diabetes and encourage individuals, families, and communities to understand risk factors, prevention, early detection, and long-term management.

  • Community diabetes awareness
  • Health education campaigns
  • Diabetes information and education materials
  • World Diabetes Day activities
  • Family and community engagement
02
S

Diabetes Screening & Prevention

We support community-based approaches that promote diabetes risk awareness, screening, early detection, referral, and prevention.

  • Community screening activities
  • Risk-factor awareness
  • Early detection initiatives
  • Health promotion and prevention
  • Referral and linkage to appropriate care
03
P

Patient Support & Community Engagement

We seek to strengthen support for people living with diabetes and their families while encouraging meaningful community participation in diabetes-related initiatives.

  • Patient and family support
  • Community engagement
  • Diabetes self-management education
  • Support for vulnerable communities
  • Patient-centered initiatives
04
H

Healthcare Professional Education

We contribute to strengthening the knowledge and capacity of healthcare professionals involved in diabetes prevention, detection, treatment, and long-term care.

  • Diabetes education and training
  • Professional capacity building
  • Clinical education initiatives
  • Knowledge-sharing activities
  • Healthcare workforce engagement
05
R

Research & Data

SDF promotes research and evidence generation to improve understanding of diabetes and strengthen the design and implementation of effective programs in Somalia.

  • Diabetes research
  • Community and clinical studies
  • Data collection and analysis
  • Evidence-informed program development
  • Research collaboration and dissemination
06
A

Advocacy & Health-System Strengthening

We advocate for stronger diabetes prevention and care systems and seek partnerships that can improve access, quality, sustainability, and coordination of diabetes services.

  • Health policy advocacy
  • Health-system strengthening
  • Partnership development
  • Access to diabetes care and supplies
  • Resource mobilization
Our Approach

Working across the diabetes journey

Effective diabetes action requires more than one intervention. SDF brings complementary activities together to support prevention, early detection, better care, evidence, and stronger communities.

1
Prevent Promote healthy choices, risk awareness, and prevention of type 2 diabetes where possible.
2
Detect Encourage screening, early diagnosis, and appropriate referral to healthcare services.
3
Support Help people living with diabetes and their families access information, education, and support.
4
Strengthen Build knowledge, research capacity, partnerships, and stronger systems for diabetes prevention and care.
Collaboration

No single organization can address diabetes alone.

SDF believes that sustainable progress depends on collaboration between communities, healthcare professionals, government institutions, civil society, researchers, development partners, and people living with diabetes.

We seek responsible partnerships that strengthen the reach, quality, and sustainability of diabetes programs in Somalia.

✓
Healthcare Institutions Collaboration on screening, education, referrals, clinical capacity, and community health initiatives.
✓
Government & Public Health Partners Supporting stronger diabetes prevention, policy, coordination, and health-system responses.
✓
Researchers & Academic Institutions Promoting evidence generation, research collaboration, training, and knowledge dissemination.
✓
Communities & People Living With Diabetes Ensuring that programs remain responsive to real community and patient needs.
