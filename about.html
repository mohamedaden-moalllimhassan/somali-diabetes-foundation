About the Somali Diabetes Foundation

Working toward a healthier Somalia through diabetes prevention, awareness, management, research, advocacy, professional education, and community support.

Who we are

Building a stronger response to diabetes in Somalia

The Somali Diabetes Foundation (SDF) is a nonprofit organization dedicated to improving diabetes prevention, awareness, care, education, research, advocacy, and community support in Somalia.

SDF recognizes that diabetes requires a comprehensive response that goes beyond treatment alone. Prevention, early detection, appropriate treatment, regular follow-up, patient education, family support, complication prevention, and stronger health systems all have an important role.

The Foundation works toward bringing together communities, healthcare professionals, institutions, government stakeholders, researchers, humanitarian organizations, and other partners around a stronger national response to diabetes.

Our Purpose

To contribute to a stronger diabetes response in Somalia by connecting prevention, awareness, care, education, patient support, research, advocacy, and collaboration.

Our Commitment

SDF is committed to community-centered work, evidence-informed approaches, professional capacity building, partnership, and responsible use of resources.

Our direction

Mission & Vision

Our mission and vision guide the Foundation's work toward better diabetes prevention, care, support, and health outcomes in Somalia.

What guides our work

Our Core Goals

SDF's work is organized around interconnected areas designed to strengthen diabetes prevention, care, knowledge, and support.

1

Public Awareness & Education

Increase public understanding of diabetes, its risk factors, symptoms, prevention, management, and complications.

2

Screening & Prevention

Promote risk assessment, community screening, early detection, healthy lifestyles, and timely intervention.

3

Healthcare Professional Training

Strengthen the capacity of doctors, nurses, community health workers, and other healthcare professionals involved in diabetes care.

4

Policy & Health-System Strengthening

Support stronger diabetes policies, accessible treatment, and integration of diabetes care into health services.

5

Research & Data

Generate and use research and data to better understand diabetes and inform health programs, policy, and practice.

6

Patient & Community Support

Support people living with diabetes and their families through education, community engagement, counseling, and peer support.

7

Fundraising & Resource Mobilization

Mobilize resources through grants, donations, partnerships, and mission-aligned activities to sustain diabetes programs.

Idea conceived

Officially founded

20 November 2024

The idea was transformed into a formal organization committed to contributing to a stronger diabetes response in Somalia.

Our story

From one encounter to a national vision

The founding story of SDF began during an ordinary night-shift commute, when the Founder, a medical doctor, met a young boy living with diabetes who had a dialysis catheter and was experiencing serious financial barriers to essential care.

That encounter raised a fundamental question: could a different outcome have been possible through earlier diabetes education, prevention, diagnosis, appropriate treatment, monitoring, and comprehensive support?

The experience became the seed of an idea: building an organization that could contribute to diabetes awareness, prevention, early detection, patient support, professional education, advocacy, research, partnerships, and stronger diabetes care in Somalia.

From one encounter came one idea. From one idea came one foundation. From one foundation comes a vision for a healthier Somalia.
