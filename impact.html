Our Impact

From commitment to action

From community awareness and screening to education, partnerships, advocacy and public health engagement, the Somali Diabetes Foundation is building a growing response to diabetes in Somalia.

Our Progress

Building impact step by step

SDF began as a response to a real human need and has progressively developed into an organization working across diabetes awareness, prevention, screening, patient education, professional engagement, partnerships and advocacy.

Our approach is community-centered: bringing diabetes information and screening closer to people while connecting community needs with healthcare professionals and institutions.

Impact with integrity

We believe impact should be demonstrated through documented activities, meaningful partnerships, transparent reporting and measurable results. As SDF grows, we will continue strengthening our monitoring, evaluation and reporting systems.
