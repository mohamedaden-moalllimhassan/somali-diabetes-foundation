Our Impact

From commitment to action

From community awareness and screening to education, partnerships, advocacy and public health engagement, the Somali Diabetes Foundation is building a growing response to diabetes in Somalia.

Our Progress

Building impact step by step

SDF began as a response to a real human need and has progressively developed into an organization working across diabetes awareness, prevention, screening, patient education, professional engagement, partnerships and advocacy.

Our approach is community-centered: bringing diabetes information and screening closer to people while connecting community needs with healthcare professionals and institutions.

Impact with integrity

We believe impact should be demonstrated through documented activities, meaningful partnerships, transparent reporting and measurable results. As SDF grows, we will continue strengthening our monitoring, evaluation and reporting systems.

Our Journey

A growing movement for diabetes action

SDF's development has been gradual, moving from community-level awareness toward broader professional, institutional and national engagement.

2024
20 August 2024

The idea begins

The idea for SDF was conceived following a clinical encounter that highlighted the devastating intersection of diabetes, poverty and limited access to care.

SDF
20 November 2024

Official foundation of SDF

The Somali Diabetes Foundation was officially founded, beginning its organizational journey with a commitment to diabetes prevention, awareness, care, education, research, advocacy and support.

2025
Early community action

Awareness and screening reach communities

SDF's community approach focused particularly on vulnerable communities, bringing diabetes education and screening closer to where people live.

2025
Mission expansion

Mission expands

SDF expanded its work across public education, community outreach, screening, prevention, patient education, advocacy, professional engagement, partnerships and public diabetes campaigns.

2025–2026

Partnerships strengthen the response

SDF developed institutional engagement including collaboration connected to Kaamil Specialist Hospital and diabetes-focused professional engagement with educational institutions.

Looking Ahead

Making impact increasingly measurable

As SDF continues to grow, we are strengthening systems that allow us to document activities, learn from implementation and measure meaningful results.

Our goal is to move from documenting what we do toward demonstrating how our work contributes to better diabetes awareness, prevention, care and support.

Areas we aim to monitor

  • Communities reached through awareness activities
  • People participating in diabetes screening
  • Diabetes education and training activities
  • Healthcare professionals engaged
  • Institutional and community partnerships
  • Research and evidence-generation activities
  • Patient referrals and support pathways
  • Outcomes, lessons and areas for improvement
