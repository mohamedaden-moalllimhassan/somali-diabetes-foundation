WHO WE ARE Building a stronger response to diabetes in Somalia The Somali Diabetes Foundation (SDF) is a non-governmental, non-profit and non-political organization committed to preventing, raising awareness of, and managing diabetes in Somalia. Through education, advocacy, research, community engagement and healthcare interventions, SDF works to improve the quality of life of people affected by diabetes and contribute to a healthier Somalia. Learn more about SDF → Our Mission To lead efforts in diabetes prevention, awareness, management and advocacy across Somalia through education, support programs and partnerships, ensuring better healthcare access and outcomes for people living with diabetes. Our Vision To create a healthier Somali society with reduced diabetes prevalence and improved quality of life for individuals living with diabetes. WHAT WE DO Our priority areas Our work brings together prevention, care, education, research, advocacy and community engagement. EDUCATION Public Awareness & Education Community education on diabetes causes, prevention, symptoms, healthy lifestyles and management. PREVENTION Screening & Prevention Promoting risk assessment, screening, early detection and timely referral for intervention. CAPACITY Healthcare Professional Training Supporting training, workshops and continuing education to strengthen diabetes care capacity. ADVOCACY Policy & Health-System Strengthening Working with stakeholders to strengthen diabetes-related policies, services and access to care. RESEARCH Research & Data Supporting research and data collection that can inform policy, programs and improved diabetes care. SUPPORT Patient & Community Support Supporting people and families through community engagement, peer support, counselling and health education. RESOURCES Fundraising & Resource Mobilization Mobilizing resources and building partnerships to support sustainable diabetes-focused initiatives. SDF began with an encounter that highlighted the human cost of diabetes, poverty and limited access to care. The idea was conceived on 20 August 2024 and the Foundation was officially founded on 20 November 2024. From one encounter came one idea. From one idea came one foundation. From one foundation comes a vision for a healthier Somalia. Read the full founding history EVIDENCE & LEARNING Research & knowledge SDF is committed to promoting research and data that can strengthen diabetes prevention, care, policy and health programs in Somalia. Research priorities • Diabetes prevention and awareness • Risk factors and early detection • Access to diabetes treatment and care • Diabetes complications and patient support • Community and health-system research • Evidence for policy and program development Building an evidence base We aim to support responsible research, data collection, collaboration with academic and health institutions, and dissemination of findings that can contribute to better diabetes outcomes.
