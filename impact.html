Impact · SDF

Turning commitment into meaningful impact.

SDF works to strengthen diabetes awareness, prevention, screening, education, patient support, research and health-system engagement across Somalia.

Our Impact

Building impact through practical action.

SDF's approach brings together community engagement, healthcare capacity, advocacy, research and partnerships. The goal is to contribute to better diabetes prevention, earlier detection, improved care and stronger support for people living with diabetes.

✓

From Awareness to Action

SDF seeks to connect community needs with professional knowledge and institutional action, creating a practical pathway toward a stronger diabetes response in Somalia.

Areas of Impact

Where our work can make a difference.

SDF's impact is organized around the Foundation's core goals and program areas.

01

Diabetes Awareness

Increasing knowledge and understanding of diabetes among communities and the wider public.

  • Diabetes education and awareness.
  • Healthy lifestyle information.
  • Community outreach and engagement.
02

Prevention & Early Detection

Supporting prevention and early identification of diabetes and people at increased risk.

  • Community screening.
  • Risk assessment.
  • Early detection and referral.
03

Better Diabetes Management

Supporting people living with diabetes with knowledge, resources and access to appropriate care.

  • Patient education.
  • Self-management information.
  • Support for improved healthcare access.
04

Healthcare Capacity

Contributing to stronger diabetes knowledge and skills among healthcare professionals.

  • Professional training.
  • Workshops and education.
  • Clinical knowledge and best practices.
05

Research & Evidence

Supporting research and data collection to improve understanding of diabetes and healthcare challenges.

  • Epidemiological research.
  • Diabetes data collection.
  • Evidence-informed interventions.
06

Policy & Health Systems

Working with stakeholders to strengthen diabetes policies, services and access to care.

  • Policy advocacy.
  • Health-system engagement.
  • Improved access to diabetes services.
