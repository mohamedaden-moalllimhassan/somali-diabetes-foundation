Our Story

From one encounter to a national vision

The idea behind the Somali Diabetes Foundation began with a powerful encounter that highlighted the human cost of diabetes and limited access to essential care.

From that experience came a commitment to build an organization focused on awareness, prevention, screening, treatment, patient support and research.
