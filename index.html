Who we are

Building a stronger response to diabetes in Somalia

The Somali Diabetes Foundation (SDF) is a non-governmental, non-profit and non-political organization committed to preventing, raising awareness of, and managing diabetes in Somalia.

Our Mission

To lead efforts in diabetes prevention, awareness, management and advocacy across Somalia through education, support programs and partnerships, ensuring better healthcare access and outcomes for people living with diabetes.

Learn more about SDF →

Our Vision

To create a healthier Somali society with reduced diabetes prevalence and improved quality of life for individuals living with diabetes.

What we do

Our priority areas

Our work brings together prevention, care, education, research, advocacy and community engagement.

01

Public Awareness & Education

Community education on diabetes causes, prevention, symptoms, healthy lifestyles and management.

02

Screening & Prevention

Promoting risk assessment, screening, early detection and timely referral for intervention.

03

Healthcare Professional Training

Supporting training, workshops and continuing education to strengthen diabetes care capacity.

04

Policy & Health-System Strengthening

Working with stakeholders to strengthen diabetes-related policies, services and access to care.

05

Research & Data

Supporting research and data collection that can inform policy, programs and improved diabetes care.

06

Patient & Community Support

Supporting people and families through community engagement, peer support, counselling and health education.

07

Fundraising & Resource Mobilization

Mobilizing resources and building partnerships to support sustainable diabetes-focused initiatives.

Our founding story

From one encounter to a national vision.

SDF began with an encounter that highlighted the human cost of diabetes, poverty and limited access to care. The idea was conceived on 20 August 2024 and the Foundation was officially founded on 20 November 2024.

From one encounter came one idea. From one idea came one foundation. From one foundation comes a vision for a healthier Somalia.

Evidence & Learning

Research & knowledge

SDF is committed to promoting research and data that can strengthen diabetes prevention, care, policy and health programs in Somalia.

Research priorities

  • Diabetes prevention and awareness
  • Risk factors and early detection
  • Access to diabetes treatment and care
  • Diabetes complications and patient support
  • Community and health-system research
  • Evidence for policy and program development

Building an evidence base

We aim to support responsible research, data collection, collaboration with academic and health institutions, and dissemination of findings that can contribute to better diabetes outcomes.
