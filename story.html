From One Encounter to a National Vision

The founding story of the Somali Diabetes Foundation

From One Boy to a National Movement

Every organization has a beginning. For the Somali Diabetes Foundation (SDF), that beginning was not a boardroom, a large grant, or a fully funded project. It was an encounter with a young boy on an ordinary night-shift commute.

The Encounter

The Encounter That Changed Everything

During a journey to a night-shift duty, the Founder, a medical doctor, met a young boy who boarded a public minibus at Sey Biyano Junction.

The Founder noticed gauze covering a central venous catheter in the boy's neck. The boy explained that it was for dialysis and that he was living with diabetes.

The conversation revealed that the boy had lost both his parents and was living with his uncle in an internally displaced persons' community around Mogadishu. His uncle struggled financially to cover dialysis costs, and the boy sometimes missed dialysis sessions because of this hardship.

The Question

From One Patient to a National Problem

For the Founder, this was more than a conversation. It was a story of vulnerability, loss, poverty, chronic illness and limited access to essential healthcare.

One question remained:

Could this young boy's life have been different if he had received proper diabetes education, early diagnosis, appropriate treatment, regular monitoring and comprehensive support before his complications became severe?

20 August 2024

The Birth of the Idea

On 20 August 2024, the idea that would become the Somali Diabetes Foundation was born.

The conviction was simple: people living with diabetes in Somalia deserve knowledge, care, support and the opportunity to live healthier lives and avoid preventable complications.

The vision included awareness, prevention, early detection, patient education, improved access to care, complication prevention, professional education, advocacy, community engagement, research and partnerships.

August–November 2024

From an Idea to an Organization

The Founder began turning the vision into an organization, addressing fundamental questions about its purpose, mission, priorities and the communities it would serve.

SDF was envisioned as a platform bringing together patients, families, healthcare professionals, communities, government, civil society, universities, development partners and international organizations.

20 November 2024

Official Founding of SDF

The Somali Diabetes Foundation was officially founded on 20 November 2024.

SDF did not begin with abundant resources. It began with determination. The Founder and early members contributed what they could to establish the organization and begin its work.

Early Development

Building SDF From the Ground Up

The early period focused on establishing SDF's organizational identity, direction, collaborations and practical ways to reach people who needed diabetes information and support.

Vulnerable communities, including internally displaced communities around Mogadishu, became an early priority.

2025

Expanding the Mission

During 2025, SDF continued expanding its work through diabetes awareness, community outreach, screening, prevention, patient education, advocacy, professional engagement, partnerships and public diabetes campaigns.

SDF also participated in the Mogadishu Global Diabetes Walk, connecting diabetes awareness in Somalia with a broader international initiative.

Partnerships

Strengthening Diabetes Care

As SDF developed, it began forming partnerships to strengthen diabetes services. One important partnership was established with Dr. Sumaid and Kaamil Specialist Hospital, through which SDF began preparing to establish an SDF Diabetes Clinic at Kaamil Specialist Hospital.

SDF also expanded engagement with educational institutions and healthcare professionals, including a training program at Hayat Medical University.

2026

Toward a National Platform

By 2026, SDF had continued developing from a newly established organization into an increasingly visible platform for diabetes awareness, prevention, advocacy, education and community engagement.

The journey continues to connect community needs with professional knowledge and institutional action.

The Founder's Leadership

The Founder and CEO has been central to SDF's establishment and development from its earliest days. The Foundation was created without significant initial resources, with a commitment to begin despite the challenges.

This experience shaped a leadership philosophy grounded in:

Service · Compassion · Persistence · Professionalism · Community Responsibility · Partnership · Impact

The Meaning of SDF

For the Founder, SDF represents more than an organization. It represents a response to a problem witnessed firsthand and a belief that people living with diabetes should not face devastating consequences without adequate knowledge, care and support.

SDF's purpose is both medical and human: disease prevention and treatment, but also dignity, knowledge, opportunity, advocacy and hope.

The Future — Toward a Healthier Somalia

SDF aspires to become a leading institution for diabetes prevention, education, advocacy, research and care in Somalia while building meaningful connections with regional and international diabetes organizations.

SDF envisions a Somalia where diabetes awareness reaches every community, people at risk can access prevention and early screening, people living with diabetes can access continuous appropriate care, and healthcare professionals receive continuing education and training.

The Foundation also envisions stronger diabetes research, evidence-based policy, patient representation and collaboration among government, healthcare institutions, universities, civil society, communities and international partners.

From one idea came one foundation.
From one foundation comes a vision for a healthier Somalia.
