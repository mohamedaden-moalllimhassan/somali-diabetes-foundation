Who We Are

Building a stronger response to diabetes in Somalia.

The Somali Diabetes Foundation is a nonprofit organization working to improve diabetes awareness, prevention, early detection, management, research, and support for people affected by diabetes and their families.

SDF brings together community engagement, healthcare professional development, research, advocacy, and partnerships to contribute to a healthier Somalia.

Our Story

From one encounter to a national vision.

The Somali Diabetes Foundation grew from an encounter that highlighted the human cost of diabetes and the challenges faced by people who need ongoing care.

What began as an idea on 20 August 2024 became the Somali Diabetes Foundation on 20 November 2024, with a vision to contribute to a healthier Somalia.
