What Your Support Can Help

Contributions can help SDF strengthen its work in diabetes prevention, education, care, research and community support.

Public Awareness

Supporting diabetes education and public awareness activities in communities.

Screening & Prevention

Helping promote early detection, risk awareness and prevention of diabetes complications.

Patient Support

Supporting initiatives that improve access to diabetes information, guidance and community support.

Healthcare Education

Contributing to professional education and capacity building for healthcare workers.

Research

Supporting research and data collection to strengthen evidence-based diabetes care in Somalia.

Health Advocacy

Helping advance stronger diabetes policies, health systems and community engagement.

Supporting SDF means supporting sustainable diabetes care.

The Somali Diabetes Foundation is committed to responsible, transparent and accountable use of resources in support of its mission and programs.

Our commitment

  • Resources are directed toward SDF's mission and approved activities.
  • Donations are handled through official SDF channels.
  • Program activities and results will be reported as the organization grows.
  • SDF seeks responsible partnerships and resource mobilization for sustainable impact.

How to make a bank transfer

Choose a bank
