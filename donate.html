Support SDF

Support better diabetes care in Somalia.

Your contribution can help advance diabetes awareness, prevention, screening, patient support, research, education, and stronger diabetes care across Somalia.

Make a meaningful contribution

Somali Diabetes Foundation (SDF) works to strengthen the prevention, detection, management, and understanding of diabetes in Somalia.

Donations help support the Foundation's mission and its efforts to build stronger communities, improve diabetes awareness, support patients and families, strengthen healthcare capacity, and promote evidence-based action.

You can support SDF through any of the official bank accounts listed below.

Every contribution matters

Whether large or small, your support contributes to the broader effort to improve diabetes care and reduce the burden of diabetes in Somalia.

Donation Transparency

Somali Diabetes Foundation is committed to responsible and transparent use of donated resources. Donors are encouraged to retain their transfer receipt for their records and may contact SDF for donation-related enquiries or confirmation.

How to make a bank transfer

A simple three-step process for supporting SDF through bank transfer.

1

Choose a bank

Select one of the official Somali Diabetes Foundation bank accounts listed above.

2

Make your transfer

Use the relevant account number or IBAN and follow your bank's instructions to complete the transfer.

3

Keep your receipt

Retain your transaction receipt. For donation-related enquiries or confirmation, contact the Somali Diabetes Foundation.
