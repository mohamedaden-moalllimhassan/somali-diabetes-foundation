Ways to Engage

There are several ways individuals, organizations and institutions can contribute to the mission of SDF.

Partner with SDF

Work with us to strengthen diabetes awareness, prevention, screening, treatment and community health initiatives in Somalia.

Volunteer

Support community awareness activities, screening initiatives, education and other SDF programs.

Research Collaboration

Collaborate with SDF on diabetes research, noncommunicable diseases, public health, clinical education and health-system strengthening.
