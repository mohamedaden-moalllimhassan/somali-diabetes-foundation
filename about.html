About the Somali Diabetes Foundation

Working toward a healthier Somalia through diabetes prevention, awareness, management, research, advocacy and community support.

Who We Are

The Somali Diabetes Foundation (SDF) is a non-governmental, non-profit and non-political organization committed to preventing, raising awareness of, and managing diabetes in Somalia. Through education, advocacy, research and direct healthcare interventions, SDF works to improve the quality of life of individuals affected by diabetes and promote a healthier future for all.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To lead efforts in diabetes prevention, awareness, management and advocacy across Somalia through education, support programs and partnerships, ensuring better healthcare access and outcomes for people living with diabetes.

Our Vision

To create a healthier Somali society with reduced diabetes prevalence and improved quality of life for individuals living with diabetes.

Our Core Goals

SDF works across multiple areas to strengthen diabetes prevention, care and support in Somalia.

1. Increase Public Awareness Enhance community knowledge about diabetes causes, prevention, symptoms and management.
2. Improve Diabetes Management Support people living with diabetes through access to appropriate care, resources and self-management information.
3. Strengthen Healthcare Capacity Train and equip healthcare professionals with up-to-date knowledge and skills in diabetes prevention and treatment.
4. Enhance Access to Medication Advocate for affordable and accessible diabetes medications, including insulin and essential medical supplies.
5. Promote Research & Data Collection Conduct studies on diabetes prevalence, risk factors and healthcare challenges to inform policies and interventions.
6. Policy Advocacy Work with government authorities and stakeholders to strengthen diabetes-related policies and healthcare services.
7. Support Community Initiatives Engage communities in diabetes prevention through physical activity, healthy lifestyle guidance and support groups.
8. Build Partnerships Collaborate with local and international organizations, healthcare institutions and humanitarian agencies.

Our Commitment

SDF is committed to a comprehensive response to diabetes that connects communities, healthcare professionals, institutions and partners.

Our Founding Story

SDF began with an encounter that changed everything. A young person's experience with diabetes and dialysis led to a question about what could have been different through earlier education, diagnosis, appropriate treatment and continuous support.

The idea was conceived on 20 August 2024 and the Somali Diabetes Foundation was officially founded on 20 November 2024.
