Our Story

From one encounter to a national vision.

The Somali Diabetes Foundation grew from a personal encounter that highlighted the challenges faced by people living with diabetes and other chronic conditions in Somalia.

From that experience came a commitment to build an organization focused on awareness, prevention, screening, care, education, research and patient support.
